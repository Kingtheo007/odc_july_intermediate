About ODC July Intermediate

The ODC July Intermediate website is a showcase of projects and achievements by participants of the ODC July intermediate coding classes. This platform highlights the collaborative spirit, creativity, and technical skills developed during the course. Each card on the homepage represents a participant or a project, providing a glimpse into the diverse talents within the group.
